Unjamly Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

This policy explains how Unjamly: Parking Puzzle, package com.webillium.unjamly, provided by Webillium, handles information.

Game progress

Unjamly does not require an account. Your highest unlocked level, coins and sound preference are stored locally. An unfinished attempt restarts when you reopen the game. All 100 levels can be played offline. There are no purchases, social features, user uploads or separate developer analytics services.

Advertising and information processed

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between levels. Rewarded ads provide one extra heart after successful completion, at most once per attempt. Free retry and hints do not require an ad. Ads and consent services require internet access.

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, advertising ID, app set ID and other applicable device/account identifiers. These are used for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. The game does not request GPS/location permission or provide your name or email to the SDK.

See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps and Google's SDK data disclosure. Google and advertising partners process information according to the applicable settings, consent and their policies; their retention periods may differ from local game storage.

Your advertising choices

Google's User Messaging Platform checks applicable privacy requirements at app launch and presents a consent or privacy message when required. The game requests ads only when the SDK reports that requests are permitted. Open the game menu, choose Privacy policy, then Ad privacy choices to revisit available choices. This button is enabled when the privacy platform requires an entry point for your region. You can decline consent or change your choices where offered. Non-personalized or limited ads may still be available, depending on the choice and applicable requirements. If ads are unavailable, you can continue playing and retry for free.

Android's privacy/ads settings also let you reset or delete the advertising ID. For privacy rights concerning information held by Google, use Google's privacy controls and contact channels. For information held by Webillium, contact us below.

Technical components and security

Unjamly is built with Unity and includes Google's Play Asset Delivery compatibility library. All game content ships in the base installation; the game does not request asset downloads. Internet, network-state and advertising-ID access support advertising. SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS according to Google's disclosure. Local data is protected by Android's app sandbox; we do not claim separate encryption of game progress.

Google Play and your operating system may separately process installation, security and diagnostic information under their own policies.

Support inquiries and website

If you email office@webillium.com, Webillium receives your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and investigate issues. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve your request and maintain related support records; you may request deletion through the same address, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Support and privacy links open in your external browser. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information such as IP address and browser details under its privacy statement. The Unjamly website adds no advertising, tracking cookies or analytics scripts.

Retention, deletion and device changes

Local progress remains until you clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. This release disables Android app backup. Progress is not designed to transfer to another device. There is no online Unjamly account to delete.

Sharing

Advertising information is processed by Google and relevant advertising partners as described above. Webillium does not operate a player-account database. Support information may be processed by the email providers needed to receive and answer your message.

Young users

The game is designed for a general teen and adult puzzle audience, not specifically for children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information by email can contact us to request deletion.

Artwork and AI

Some static artwork was created with AI-assisted tools during development. The app does not offer an AI chat or image-generation feature and does not send player content to an AI service.

Changes and contact

We update this policy and Google Play disclosures when the app’s data practices change.
