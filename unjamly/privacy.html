Unjamly Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

This policy explains how Unjamly: Parking Puzzle, package com.webillium.unjamly, provided by Webillium, handles information.

Offline game and local progress

Unjamly does not require an account. Version 1.0.0 does not collect or transmit personal information or gameplay activity to Webillium. It includes no advertising SDK, analytics service, purchases, social features, location tracking or user uploads.

The game stores your highest unlocked level, earned coins and sound preference locally in app storage. These values stay on your device; there is no Webillium cloud save or account. An unfinished attempt restarts when you reopen the game.

Technical components

Unjamly is built with Unity. The Android build includes Google's Play Asset Delivery compatibility library because Unity checks for its classes at startup. All game content is included in the base installation; this version does not request additional asset downloads. The release app does not request Android internet, network-state or advertising-ID permissions. It does not send developer analytics or crash reports.

Google Play and your operating system may separately process installation, security, diagnostic or other information under their own settings and privacy policies. Webillium does not control those platform services.

Support inquiries and website

If you email office@webillium.com, Webillium receives your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and investigate issues. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve your request and maintain related support records; you may request deletion through the same address, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Support and privacy links open in your external browser. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information such as IP address and browser details under its privacy statement. The Unjamly website adds no advertising, tracking cookies or analytics scripts.

Retention, deletion and device changes

Local progress remains until you clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. This release disables Android app backup. Progress is not designed to transfer to another device. There is no online Unjamly account to delete.

Sharing and security

Webillium does not sell game-user data or share gameplay data with advertising partners. Local app data is protected by Android's application sandbox. Support information may be processed by the email providers needed to receive and answer your message; access is limited to handling support. We do not claim that local game progress is encrypted independently of your device's protections.

Young users

The game is designed for a general teen and adult puzzle audience, not specifically for children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information by email can contact us to request deletion.

Artwork and AI

Some static artwork was created with AI-assisted tools during development. The app does not offer an AI chat or image-generation feature and does not send player content to an AI service.

Changes and contact

If future versions introduce ads, online features or other data practices, this policy and the Google Play disclosures will be updated before those features are released.

Privacy contact: Webillium · office@webillium.com
